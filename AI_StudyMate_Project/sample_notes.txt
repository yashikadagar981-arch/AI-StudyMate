Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science focused on building systems that perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Natural Language Processing (NLP) helps computers work with human language, including classification, summarization, and question answering. Generative AI models create new content such as text, images, or code based on patterns learned during training. Prompt engineering is the practice of designing clear instructions and context to guide an AI model toward a useful response. Sentiment analysis identifies the emotional tone of text and may classify it as positive, negative, or neutral. Responsible AI includes fairness, reliability, privacy, security, transparency, inclusiveness, and accountability. AI systems can make mistakes, so users should verify important outputs and avoid entering sensitive personal information.
